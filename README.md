# Nexus

`nexus` is the universal library for Nexus components. Beside it in this
workspace, `nexus-exchange`, `nexus-querying` and `nexus-serving` are the
serving every Nexus shares: its socket names and lanes, the querying end of a
connection, and the daemon's listeners, meta and public exchange loops and
emission lane, which a Nexus fills with its own bodies. `nexus` owns the standard
ontology shared by every long-running Nexus while component repositories own
their domain configuration, signal contracts, Sema records, and effects.

The first shared type is `ConfigurationState<C>`. A fresh Sema seeds it from
the executable's built-in default. Ordinary Configure may update it while no
meta Configure has occurred. A successful meta Configure closes ordinary
Configure, and only a meta reversal opens it again.

The stored configuration is desired state. A running Nexus keeps the startup
snapshot active and applies newly persisted socket settings on its next
zero-argument start. Its stable Sema discovery path is executable-owned rather
than mutable configuration, so configuration cannot redirect a Nexus away from
its domain state.

`SocketAuthority` and `Permissive` are the second shared kind: the mode a socket
is bound with and the peers that authority admits. The ordinary socket admits
whoever the filesystem let through; the privileged socket is bound `0600` and
answers only the user it belongs to.

`Situation` is the observed half of that metadata: the store file a Nexus
actually opened and the sockets it actually bound, written at bind and never
read back as configuration. A store opened anywhere other than where its own
record says it lives is a copy, and its recorded sockets belong to another
process.

`Publisher<State, Change>` is the subscription half: the one current state a
Nexus publishes, read from the Publisher and kept nowhere else, and the
streams that follow it. `publish` folds a change into the state with
`signal::Following::after` and hands it to every open stream, answering with how many
it `Reached`; `open` yields a `signal::Stream` that opens on the state as it
stands, carries each later change in order, hands a subscriber that fell more
than the `Capacity` behind the state anew as `Lagged`, and finishes when the
Publisher is dropped. `resize` sets the `Capacity` on the mutable Publisher and
every open stream follows it, answering with how many it `Reached`. No subscriber slows the Nexus or another subscriber.

The vocabulary the Publisher speaks, `Capacity`, `Reached`, `Subscribable`
and `Publishing`, is declared in `ethos/library.ethos` and
generated by ethos-zero into `src/generated/library.rs`, which is committed;
the build script refuses a generation that differs from the source. With the
`datom` feature the generated kinds also derive the Datom codec's traits.
`Following`, the fold of a change into the state that the subscriber applies
as the Publisher does, is signal's: the Publisher's `State: Following` bound
asserts that the state a `signal::Stream` carries bears it.
